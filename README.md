# The Field Researcher's Dilemma

An interactive seminar simulation in qualitative case selection, designed for advanced qualitative methods teaching in peace and conflict research and political science.

Students play the role of a researcher designing a comparative study of electoral violence. They are presented with eight districts and must select two for an in-depth comparison under incomplete information, weighing design quality, ethical constraints, and practical feasibility. The simulation then walks them through design reflection, single-case analysis, and a series of fieldwork crises that force them to confront the limits of their chosen design.
